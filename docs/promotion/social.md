## LinkedIn

**标题：**

```
We built an open directory of Chinese manufacturers — searchable by AI agents
```

**正文：**

Sourcing teams spend days finding upstream manufacturers in China — Baidu noise,
marketplace ad spam, trade-show schedules. What if the first step could be a
one-line query to your AI assistant?

BeaconMFG is an open-source, agent-searchable directory of Chinese manufacturers:

- 1,739 companies across 8 categories (CNC machining, sheet metal, injection molding,
  die casting, electronic components, surface treatment, standard parts, raw materials)
- Full English mirror for global procurement teams
- Every listing: company, product keywords, region, public contact, data source
- Compliance-first: public info only, personal mobiles masked, no scraping of
  B2B platforms, listings removable on request

Use cases:
- Procurement agents: reduce first-pass sourcing from days to minutes
- Startups prototyping: quickly shortlist CNC/injection molding shops by region
- B2B software: embed a free supplier lookup layer in your workflow

It's free and open (MIT code, CC BY-NC data). Contributions welcome — especially
data verification and new categories.

github.com/eiry16/beacon-mfg
